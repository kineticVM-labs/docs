KineticVM on Monad is four addresses a device talks to, plus two ERC-8004 registries it shares with every other agent on the chain. The device never holds a special role in any of them. It holds a key that the registry has bound to an agent, and every contract checks that binding before it accepts a signature.

| Contract | Job |
| --- | --- |
| [KineticRegistry](/contracts/kinetic-registry) | Mints the agent NFT into the owner's wallet and binds one device key to it. |
| [DeviceVault](/contracts/device-vault) | Holds MON per agent and enforces caps, the allowlist, and pause. |
| [KineticAttestor](/contracts/kinetic-attestor) | Checks a device-signed action proof and writes an ERC-8004 validation. |
| [ERC-8004 registries](/contracts/erc-8004) | The public identity and validation registries. KineticVM does not deploy these. |

Addresses, deployment transactions, and explorers are on [Deployments](/contracts/deployments).

## How a claim uses all three

<Steps>
  <Step title="KineticRegistry.claim">
    Mints an ERC-8004 agent to the caller, checks the device's claim ticket, and calls `DeviceVault.bindLimits` with the starting caps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="IdentityRegistry.setApprovalForAll">
    The owner approves `KineticAttestor`. The registry cannot do this. It only holds the NFT for the duration of `claim`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="DeviceVault.deposit">
    Anyone can fund the agent. The device spends through `pay` and `topUpGas`. The owner recovers funds with `withdraw`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="KineticAttestor.attest">
    The device signs an action proof. The attestor checks the active device and the owner approval, then writes response `100` to the Validation Registry.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rules that hold in every contract

- The owner is always `ownerOf` on the Identity Registry. KineticVM does not keep a second copy that can drift.
- The device key is never the NFT owner, and no KineticVM contract approves it as an operator.
- Raising a cap, adding a recipient, or unpausing requires the owner wallet or an EIP-712 signature from it. Tightening and pausing are direct owner calls.
- `activeDevice` reverts when the NFT has moved, so a sold machine cannot keep spending on the previous owner's terms.
- The attestor never calls `giveFeedback`. Reputation is a separate ERC-8004 surface, and this deployment does not write it.

<Warning>
  These contracts are deployed on Monad testnet and have not been audited. Treat balances as testnet MON. Mainnet is refused by the runtime until a mainnet deployment exists.
</Warning>

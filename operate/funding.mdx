A claimed device has two pools of MON, and they serve different purposes.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="The vault account" icon="vault">
    Held by `DeviceVault` under the agent id. This is the device's budget. Every MON that leaves it passes the caps, the allowlist, and the pause.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The device address" icon="cpu">
    A normal balance on the device key's address. It only pays transaction fees. The device refills it from the vault, never above 5 MON.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Deposit

Anyone can fund any claimed agent. Depositing does not give the depositor any rights over the vault.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Deposit page" icon="globe">
    Send `/deposit` in Telegram, or open the link shown in the claimed banner:

    ```text
    https://vault-deposit.kineticvm.xyz?address=0x77102fCAC7927bB64507DF82bf1ae659465B4EE5&agent=2005
    ```

    Connect any wallet on Monad testnet, enter an amount, and confirm. The page checks the agent is claimed, then calls `deposit(agentId)` with the amount as value.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="cast" icon="terminal">
    ```bash
    cast send 0x77102fCAC7927bB64507DF82bf1ae659465B4EE5 \
      "deposit(uint256)" 2005 \
      --value 2ether \
      --rpc-url https://testnet-rpc.monad.xyz \
      --account funder
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning>
  Do not send MON to the vault address with a plain transfer. The vault rejects it, because it would not know which agent to credit. Always call `deposit(agentId)`.
</Warning>

Get testnet MON from the [Monad faucet](https://faucet.monad.xyz).

## How the device pays gas

The claim page gives the device its first gas: after the claim and the attestor approval, if the device address holds less than 1 MON, the page asks the owner to send it 1 MON.

From then on, the device refills itself. Before every payment and every attestation it runs this check:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the device balance">
    Above 1 MON, nothing happens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Size a top-up">
    At or below 1 MON, it computes the largest amount that fits all of these at once: room up to 5 MON, the per-transaction cap, what is left of the daily cap, and the vault balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take it from the vault">
    If that amount lifts the device above 1 MON, it calls `topUpGas(agentId, amount)` and then sends the real transaction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or stop and tell the owner">
    If the vault itself cannot cover it, the device posts a funding link to the paired Telegram chat and the transaction does not go out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Gas top-ups are spending. They count toward the per-transaction cap and the daily window, they stop while the vault is paused, and they appear as `GasToppedUp` events.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="“the spending cap is too low to top up device gas”" icon="circle-alert">
    The vault has enough MON, but the caps are too small for one top-up to lift the device above 1 MON. This happens when the device balance has fallen well below 1 MON, for example to 0.5 MON with a 0.05 MON per-transaction cap. Send the device address a little MON directly, or raise the caps with `/limits`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="“The vault does not have enough MON for device gas”" icon="wallet">
    The vault balance cannot cover the minimum top-up. Deposit more with the link in the message.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="“the device could not take gas from the vault”" icon="circle-pause">
    The top-up transaction reverted. The most common cause is a paused vault. Resume with `/resume`, or send MON to the device address directly.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  With the quickstart defaults, a 0.05 MON per-transaction cap and a 1 MON daily cap, the device tops up in 0.05 MON steps and stays just above 1 MON. Fees on Monad are small, so this covers a large number of attestations.
</Tip>

## Paying recipients

The device pays with the `vault_pay` tool, giving a recipient and an amount in MON. Before it can pay anyone:

1. The recipient must be allowlisted. Send `/approve <address>` and sign the digest. See [Telegram owner commands](/operate/telegram#the-signing-flow).
2. The vault must not be paused.
3. The amount must fit the per-transaction cap and what is left of the daily cap.

`vault_pay` always raises an approval card on chat channels before it runs.

## Withdraw

Only the current owner can withdraw, and funds always go to the owner. A withdrawal ignores the caps and works while the vault is paused, so an owner can always recover funds from a device they no longer trust.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Withdraw page" icon="globe">
    Send `/withdraw` in Telegram and open the link:

    ```text
    https://vault-deposit.kineticvm.xyz/withdraw?address=0x77102fCAC7927bB64507DF82bf1ae659465B4EE5&agent=2005
    ```

    Connect the **owner** wallet, enter an amount, and confirm. The page refuses to continue if the connected wallet is not `ownerOfAgent(agentId)`.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="cast" icon="terminal">
    ```bash
    cast send 0x77102fCAC7927bB64507DF82bf1ae659465B4EE5 \
      "withdraw(uint256,uint256)" 2005 1ether \
      --rpc-url https://testnet-rpc.monad.xyz \
      --account owner
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

MON already sent to the device address for gas is not in the vault. It belongs to the device key, and only the device can move it.
